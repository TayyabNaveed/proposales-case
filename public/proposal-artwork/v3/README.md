# Proposal cover — version 3

`cover.jpg` (2528 × 1696) is the proposal cover background: floating frosted-glass cards at three depths, a perspective floor and a violet horizon glow, in the palette of the category artwork. Generated with Google Gemini from Tayyab's prompt on 11 October 2026, then re-encoded (progressive JPEG, quality 92, full colour resolution, so the smooth glow does not band) without other edits.

Proposales places the proposal title in white over the cover and scrolls it with a parallax effect, and its PDF crops the cover to a portrait page through the centre. The centre is kept dark and open for the title (mean luminance about 33/255); detail sits at the edges and in the lower quarter. Checked with a white title in a wide web crop and a portrait A4 crop.

Earlier light covers (v1, v2) are unsuitable because the title is white.
